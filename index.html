01

Overview

Wormrise is a single-player arcade game by Eureka Digital Labs (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us"). It does not require an account, does not collect your name, email or contact details, has no in-app purchases, and does not sell your personal information for money (see Section 8). The sections below explain the limited data the app and its advertising partner handle.

02

Data we collect

We — Eureka Digital Labs (Pty) Ltd, the developer of Wormrise — do not operate our own backend server and do not directly collect or store any personal data ourselves. All gameplay data (see Section 4) stays on your device. The only data handling beyond your device is performed by the third-party services below, on their own systems, under their own privacy policies.

When the app starts it downloads a small settings file (which controls how often ads may appear) from GitHub Pages, where this policy is hosted. That request sends no personal data from the app; like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address under its own privacy policy.

04

What stays on your device

Your progress — best scores, coins, unlocked birds, upgrades, your Worm Book, streaks, daily missions and settings — and a local gameplay event log are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

The game reads your device's clock to know when it is dawn where you are (golden worms only come up between 5 and 9 AM local time). The time is only used on your device and is never sent anywhere.

If you allow notifications, the app schedules its optional dawn reminders (for example, "The golden worms are up!") locally on your device. No push service or server is involved and nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

05

App permissions

  • Internet / network state — to load ads, show the consent form and download the ad-settings file.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback during play (can be turned off in the game's settings).
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Notifications (and run-at-startup, so reminders survive a phone restart) — only for the optional dawn reminders, which are scheduled on your device. Asked for in the game, never required.
06

Children's privacy

Wormrise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads in the app are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

07

Your choices

  • Rewarded video ads are always optional — the game never requires you to watch one.
  • Change your ad consent from the in-game PRIVACY SETTINGS button (where it applies).
  • Reset or limit ad personalization from your device's ad settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored progress.
08

Your privacy rights

Who is responsible. The app is published by Eureka Digital Labs (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us"), which is responsible for this policy and for the app's own handling of personal information. Google is independently responsible for the data it processes through AdMob, under its own policies.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where the law requires it, the app only requests ads after you have made a choice in the consent form shown by Google's User Messaging Platform. Personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the PRIVACY SETTINGS button; if you decline, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads as your choices allow.

Your rights. Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. We don't run servers or keep accounts, so we hold no personal information about you beyond what stays on your device (Section 4) — but you can still contact us, and we will respond and help you reach Google for the data it holds. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in South Africa, the Information Regulator).

US state privacy rights. We do not sell your personal information for money. Showing personalised ads through Google AdMob may count as “sharing” personal information or “targeted advertising” under some US state laws (for example in California, Virginia, Colorado and Connecticut). You can opt out with the PRIVACY SETTINGS button where it is shown for your region, or by deleting or resetting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalisation in your device settings. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

International transfers. Google and GitHub may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards for such transfers (such as standard contractual clauses).

09

Data retention & deletion

Since we don't operate a backend or hold an account system, we have no server-side copy of your data to retain or delete — your local save is removed when you uninstall the app. For data held by Google (advertising ID and ad data), use Google's own account and data controls, or contact us and we'll point you to the right place.
